This is the first post on **infradawn** — a blog I've been meaning to start for a while now.

## Why start a blog?

I've always believed that writing is thinking made visible. When you work on projects, you learn a ton — but most of that knowledge stays locked in your head. I want to change that.

Here's what I plan to write about:

- **Systems and infrastructure** — the backbone of everything we build
- **Personal projects** — walkthroughs, retrospectives, and lessons learned
- **Tools and workflows** — what's working for me, and what isn't
- **Deep dives** — going beyond the surface on topics I find interesting

## What to expect

I'll keep things practical and grounded. No fluff, no clickbait. Just honest writing about the things I'm building and learning.

> The best time to start was yesterday. The second best time is now.

If you're into technology and building things, I think you'll enjoy what's coming. Stay tuned.
